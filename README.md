# 💰 PaisaKhabar Newsroom OS v2.0 - Financial News Intelligence & Alignment Engine

A next-generation, zero-dependency **Financial News Intelligence, AI Alignment & Multi-Channel Publishing OS** designed specifically for **Indian Personal Finance, Banking, EPFO/EPS-95, Stock Market, Gold/Silver, and Income Tax News**.

---

## 🌟 Key Capabilities

### 1. 📡 Live Financial Radar (Real-Time Ingestion & Clustering)
- **6 Dedicated Categories**:
  - 🏦 **Banking & Fixed Deposits (FDs)**: RBI Repo Rate, SBI/HDFC Special FD Rates, Home/Car Loan EMIs.
  - 🏛️ **EPFO, NPS & EPS-95 Pension**: Minimum pension revisions, 8th Pay Commission fitment factor math, PF interest rates.
  - 📈 **Stock Market, Nifty & IPOs**: SEBI F&O regulations, retail trader capital protection, market closing wrap-ups.
  - 🪙 **Gold, Silver & Commodities**: 24K & 22K HUID Hallmark rates, Dhanteras/festive price tracking.
  - 💰 **Income Tax, ITR & ITAT**: Wedding cash gifts, Section 56(2)(x), high-value cash transaction alerts.
  - 🇮🇳 **Govt Welfare Schemes**: PM Kisan 19th installment, DBT seeding, e-KYC status checks.
- **Smart Semantic Clustering**: Aggregates 3-5 news sources into 1 verified master story.
- **Trending Impact Score (🔥)**: Ranks stories by viral audience potential.

### 2. 🧠 AI Alignment Workbench (Zero AI Cliché & Deep Math)
- **Zero AI Clichés**: Authentic, crisp Hindi financial journalism tone without generic boilerplate.
- **3 High-CTR Headline Matrix**: Click-worthy curiosity without misleading clickbait.
- **💰 "आम आदमी की जेब पर असर" (Pocket Impact)**: Immediate consumer math and takeaway.
- **Conditional Data Tables**: Generates clean comparison tables only when numeric data exists; no forced tables.
- **Deep Financial Analysis**: Comprehensive explanations with real salary/interest/tax calculations.

### 3. 🚀 Multi-Channel 1-Click Publishing Studio
- 📝 **WordPress / Blog Article**: 750-word SEO-optimized HTML article with Schema structure and meta tags.
- 📱 **WhatsApp & Telegram Flash**: 30-second bulleted mobile bulletin with 1-tap copy and direct share.
- 🎬 **60-Second Reel / Short Script**: Hook (0-8s) ➔ Core Fact (8-45s) ➔ CTA (45-55s).
- 🖼️ **Live Canvas Visual Studio**: 16:9 4K Header Banner & 1:1 Instagram/Facebook Split Cards with 1-click PNG download.
